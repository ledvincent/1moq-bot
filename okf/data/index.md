# Data

* [lead](lead.md) - One business found for outreach, with its researched facts and their sources, its email addresses, its match keys and the owner's overrides kept apart from research
* [outbound_message](outbound-message.md) - One planned or sent outreach email, one row per lead and step (unique), carrying its text, due day and send status - planned, sending, sent, cancelled, failed or unknown
